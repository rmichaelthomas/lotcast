Check a Lotcast receipt

A receipt is the file Lotcast gave you with your draw. Paste it here to see whether it holds up. The check runs in this page, on your device: the receipt is not sent anywhere.

What does the check look at?

Six steps, in order. The first one that fails is named.

  1. Entry and promise. Your request is the one that went into the log, and the operator signed a promise to answer it in a named time slot.
  2. Log. The log's signed checkpoint contains that entry.
  3. Timestamps. Two independent time-stamp authorities stamped that checkpoint, so it existed before the random numbers did.
  4. Sources. Each public randomness beacon pulse in the receipt is genuine, and is the first one after the deadline.
  5. Outcome. The result follows from the request, the checkpoint and those numbers by a fixed method anyone can re-run.
  6. Question. If you asked a question, the receipt holds only a commitment to it; this page does not check it, because the question stays on your device. lotcast verify does.

This page checks the offline half only. It cannot ask a beacon whether it has since published a pulse for your draw, so a receipt that depends on an idle source is marked "verified except for a live check".
